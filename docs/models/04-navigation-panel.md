## Switches and Buttons

| Qty | Type |
|---:|---|
| 4× | KN3(C)-103, ON/OFF/ON |
| 1× | Rotary switch SR16, 3 position |

The rotary switch is the SOURCE selector, the four toggle switches are VHF NAV, IRS, FMC and CONTROL PANEL.
